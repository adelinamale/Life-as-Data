Decision mechanics

HOW I
MAKE DECISIONS.

The recurring logic underneath the pivots above.

01 / credibility

DO I TRUST THIS?

Claims get filtered through incentives, evidence and what survives scrutiny.

checkssource · incentives · evidenceoutputconfidence, not certainty
02 / hypothesis

WHAT HAPPENS IN REALITY?

When possible, exposure beats prolonged speculation: test the premise and let the result update it.

checksexposure · outcome · mismatchoutputretain · redirect · abandon
03 / second order

WHAT DOES SUCCESS CREATE?

A solution can work and still be wrong for the position, identity or system it creates afterward.

checkssuccess · consequence · identityoutputaccept the downstream effects
04 / leverage

WHERE DOES THE EFFECT COMPOUND?

The preferred answer is rarely a one-off achievement; it is an intervention that can travel further than the initial input.

checksproblem · bottleneck · interventionoutputscale · optionality · leverage
The common thread is not “always being strategic.” It is repeatedly moving from an assumption to a testable reality, then changing the decision criteria when the evidence changes.
